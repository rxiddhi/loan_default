# Phase 12: Final Report Structure (15 Pages)

## Page Plan
1. Cover Page
- Title, team, institute, guide, submission date.

2. Executive Summary
- Business context, objective, top outcomes, measurable impact.

3. Problem Statement and Industry Context
- Lending risk context and why default analytics matters.

4. Dataset and Scope
- Source, row/column summary, target variable, assumptions.

5. Data Audit and Quality
- Schema checks, duplicates, missingness, type checks.

6. Cleaning Methodology
- Missing strategy, outlier handling, standardization, encoding.

7. Feature Engineering and Risk Segmentation
- Derived variables and risk-band design.

8. EDA Findings
- Univariate and bivariate highlights.

9. Multivariate and Statistical Validation
- Correlation, t-tests, chi-square, logistic significance.

10. Model-Based Risk Scoring
- Probability scoring logic, risk bands, performance metrics.

11. KPI Framework
- KPI definitions, formulas, owners, governance cadence.

12. Tableau Dashboard Story
- Four dashboard narrative and interaction design.

13. Key Insights and Business Recommendations
- 12 insights + 10 action recommendations.

14. Limitations and Future Scope
- Data constraints, model constraints, next-phase improvements.

15. Contribution Matrix and Conclusion
- Member-wise contribution and final business takeaway.

## Common Report Mistakes
1. No quantitative baseline in executive summary.
2. No link between findings and recommendations.
3. Missing limitations section.
4. No member contribution traceability.
